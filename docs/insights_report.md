# Netflix Content Intelligence — Insights Report

**Snapshot:** titles added Jan 2008 – Sep 2021 · 8,807 titles · 12 raw columns
**Prepared by:** Deepanshu Garkoti

---

## Executive summary (read this out in the interview)

Netflix's catalogue is movie-heavy (69.6%) but the direction of travel is series: TV grew from
25.0% of yearly additions in 2018 to 33.7% in 2021. Acquisition peaked in 2019 at 2,016 titles
and has declined every year since (1,879 in 2020, 1,498 in 2021), which signals a shift from
volume land-grab to selective, retention-focused buying. Internationally the US still supplies
3,690 titles, but India is a firm #2 at 1,046 — served almost entirely with films (92%), while
the US slate is 25.4% series. That mismatch is the single clearest strategic gap in the data.

**Insight:** movies fill the catalogue, series carry retention. A 67% single-season rate means
two out of three commissioned shows never become a franchise.

## 4. Genre & audience

Top genres: International Movies 2,752 · Dramas 2,427 · Comedies 1,674 ·
International TV Shows 1,351 · Documentaries 869 · Action & Adventure 859.

Audience segments: Adults 4,009 (45.5%) · Teens 2,650 · Older Kids 1,150 · Kids 908 · Unrated 90.

**Insight:** the catalogue is built for adults; the kids/family tier (~23%) is thin, which is
exactly where Disney+ competes hardest.

## 5. Content freshness

Median content age at time of adding = **1 year**; **55%** of titles are added within a year of
release. Netflix is a *fresh-release* platform, not a library archive.

## 6. Talent

Directors: Rajiv Chilaka (22), Jan Suter (21), Raúl Campos (19), Suhas Kadav (16), Marcus Raboy (16).
Actors: Anupam Kher (43), Shah Rukh Khan (35), Julie Tejwani (33), Naseeruddin Shah (32),
Takahiro Sakurai (32).

**Insight:** the top-credited talent is dominated by Indian film and Indian animation, plus US
stand-up comedy directors — a good proxy for where volume deals were signed.

## 7. Seasonality

July is the heaviest month for additions and February the lightest — a Q1 content gap right
after the holiday sign-up wave, which is the highest-churn window of the year.

## 8. Recommendations

1. Commission **Indian original series** — largest non-US market, almost no series supply.
2. Rebuild the **kids/family tier** to defend against Disney+.
3. Institute a **season-1 renewal gate** to stop 67% of shows dying after one season.
4. **Smooth the Q1 calendar** — move some July volume into Feb/Mar to cut post-holiday churn.
5. Track **fresh-content % (>50%)** as a board-level KPI.

## 9. Limitations

Supply-side snapshot only — no views, revenue, or subscriber data; `country` is the production
country, not availability; ~30% of titles have no director credited; data stops Sep 2021.
